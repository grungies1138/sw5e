independently-built fan work based on the free "Star Wars 5e" tabletop
ruleset published at https://sw5e.com, itself a fan conversion of 5th
edition rules. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by

Code (module/, templates/, styles/, lang/, scripts/, sw5e.mjs, system.json)
is provided as-is. You are free to use, modify, and redistribute it for
non-commercial tabletop-gaming purposes, with attribution.

Compendium content under src/packs/ and packs/ transcribes and adapts rules
text and numeric tables from the free, publicly-available sw5e.com ruleset;
that underlying ruleset remains the work of its own contributors.
